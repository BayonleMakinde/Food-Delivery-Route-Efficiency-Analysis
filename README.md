# ⚒️ Data Forge Challenge #001

## Food Delivery Route Efficiency Analysis

## About the Challenge

Welcome to the first **Data Forge Weekly Challenge**.

For this challenge, you'll work with a food delivery dataset and investigate the factors that may affect delivery efficiency.

Your task is to **explore the data, find meaningful patterns, communicate your findings, and provide useful recommendations based on the data.**

Whether you're a beginner, intermediate, or advanced data analyst, **you can participate.**

---

## 📊 Dataset Overview

The **Food Delivery Route Efficiency Dataset** contains **200 delivery records** designed to represent real-world-style food delivery operations.

The dataset contains information about:

- Delivery distance
- Delivery time
- Traffic level
- Route length
- Delivery mode
- Weather conditions
- Order time
- Restaurant zone
- Customer zone

### Dataset Variables

| Variable | Description |
|---|---|
| `order_id` | Unique identifier for each order |
| `distance_km` | Distance covered for the delivery |
| `delivery_time_min` | Time taken to complete the delivery |
| `traffic_level` | Traffic condition during delivery |
| `route_length_km` | Length of the selected delivery route |
| `delivery_mode` | Mode used to deliver the order |
| `weather` | Weather condition during delivery |
| `order_time` | Time the order was placed |
| `restaurant_zone` | Zone where the restaurant is located |
| `customer_zone` | Zone where the customer is located |

---

## 💼 Business Problem

A food delivery company is experiencing differences in the amount of time it takes to deliver customer orders.

Management wants to understand:

> **What factors are affecting delivery time, and how can the company improve its delivery efficiency?**

You have been brought in as a data analyst to investigate the available delivery data.

Your analysis should help the company understand its delivery operations and identify patterns that may require attention.

---

## 🔎 Your Task

Explore the dataset and use your findings to understand the factors affecting delivery efficiency.

There is **no single correct approach** to this challenge.

You are encouraged to ask your own questions and investigate patterns that interest you.

### 🌱 If You're a Beginner

You can start with questions such as:

- What is the average delivery time?
- Which traffic level has the highest average delivery time?
- Which delivery mode is used most frequently?
- Which weather condition has the highest average delivery time?
- Which restaurant or customer zone has the most deliveries?

### 📈 If You're Intermediate

You can investigate questions such as:

- Does delivery distance affect delivery time?
- Does route length affect delivery time?
- How does traffic level relate to delivery time?
- Which delivery mode has shorter delivery times?
- Does order time appear to influence delivery time?
- Are there noticeable differences between zones?

### 🚀 If You're Advanced

You can take the analysis further by exploring:

- Relationships between multiple variables
- Outliers and unusual deliveries
- Delivery efficiency metrics
- Correlations between numerical variables
- Statistical analysis
- Predictive modelling
- Other patterns you discover independently

**You do not have to answer every question listed above.**

The questions are provided as starting points. We want to see your ability to **ask questions, investigate the data, and find the story behind it.**

## 📊 Expected Output

Your submission should include:

### 1. Data Analysis

Show how you explored and analysed the dataset.

### 2. Visualizations

Create meaningful visualizations that support your analysis.

**Minimum: 2 visualizations**

### 3. Key Insights

Present at least **3 meaningful insights** from your analysis.

Based on your findings, provide practical recommendations that could help the company improve its delivery efficiency.
